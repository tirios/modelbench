1. Random noise varies from reading to reading, so combining many readings over a longer window lets the positive and negative fluctuations cancel each other out.
2. That cancellation improves with the number of readings, so the spread of the combined result falls in proportion to one over the square root of the window length.
3. A systematic offset shifts every reading in the same direction by the same amount, so it survives the combination untouched and no amount of extra time will remove it.
